Soweto Country Club

A historic 18-hole golf course located right in Pimville, designed by Gary Player, featuring a modern clubhouse and fairways.

Historical Monument

Walter Sisulu Square

The site where the Freedom Charter was adopted in 1955. An essential open-air monument and heritage site in Kliptown.

Kliptown, Soweto ~4 km drive
Culture & Dining

Vilakazi Street

The famous street home to two Nobel Peace Prize winners (Nelson Mandela & Desmond Tutu), featuring restaurants and markets.

Orlando West ~7 km drive

Hector Pieterson Museum

Commemorating the June 16, 1976 Soweto Uprising, offering profound insights into South Africa's youth liberation movement.

Orlando West ~8 km drive
